# SubwaySurfersProfile

**Pre-Built Subway Surfers Profiles (JSON)** – ready-to-use, high-score, fully unlocked game profiles.  

Tired of grinding for hours in Subway Surfers? This repository gives you **pre-built profiles** that you can paste directly into your device and enjoy: high scores, all characters, boards, and achievements unlocked.  

---

## Features

- **Ready-to-use profiles** – no setup, just copy and play  
- **High scores included** – some profiles have top-runner scores  
- **All characters and boards unlocked**  
- **Achievements completed** – max progress  
- **Automatic ZIP downloads** – easy to transfer  
- **Multiple profiles** – try different styles or scores

## How to Use

1. **Download a profile** from [Releases](https://github.com/subhajit-maji/SubwaySurfersProfile/releases).  
2. **Locate your Subway Surfers profile folder** on your Android device:  

```
/Android/data/com.kiloo.subwaysurf/files/profile/
```

3. **Copy the JSON file(s)** into this folder, replacing existing files if necessary.  
4. **Open Subway Surfers** – your game now has pre-built progress, high scores, and all unlocked items.  

> ⚠️ **Backup your original profile** before replacing it. That way, you can restore your progress if needed.
